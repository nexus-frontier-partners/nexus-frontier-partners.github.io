What we do

Three services that follow the order a lender uses: first make the project real, then make it financeable, then close.

Business development

For companies with a technology or a concept and a country in mind, but not yet a sponsor, a site or a written request from the host government.

We turn a technology into a project that a lender can underwrite. That means a project company with a credible sponsor, a specified site with the rights and resource data, a written request from the host government, and a feasibility study run to international standards.

What you get

  • Project definition and ownership structure
  • Shortlist and qualification of sites and sponsors
  • Host-government engagement until the request exists in writing
  • Management of the feasibility study: we run it, engineering partners deliver it

How long it takes

Typically six to eighteen months, in stages. Each stage ends with a decision to continue, change course or stop.

How it is priced

A monthly retainer plus a fixed fee per stage. We do not take fees tied to public decisions such as budget allocations, lender approvals or tender results.

Who does the work

Our partners lead. Engineering, environmental and social, and legal specialists are brought in per project under our management.

Financial advisory

For projects that exist on paper and need capital: concessional, commercial, equity or a mix.

We design the capital structure and run the financing process with multilateral development banks, development finance institutions, climate funds, commercial banks and private investors, through to financial close.

What you get

  • Capital structure and term-sheet design
  • Blended-finance structuring: concessional windows, guarantees and first-loss capital where they apply
  • Lender and investor process, due-diligence management and negotiation
  • Documentation and conditions precedent to first drawdown

How long it takes

Nine to twenty-four months depending on the lenders involved. Concessional tranches follow committee calendars that we plan around from the start.

How it is priced

A retainer plus stage fees. A success fee applies only to private transactions and is written into the mandate.

Who we work with

We bring lenders and investors to the project; we do not represent them, and we disclose any relationship that could be read as a conflict.

Bankability readiness check

Before you commit money to a feasibility study or a financing process. Also useful when a lender has already said no and you do not know why.

A fixed-fee review in two to three weeks against the six requirements that global financial institutions apply. The result is a written report that says where the project stands, whether it is an MDB track at all, and what it would take.

What you get

  • A report against each of the six requirements
  • A clear view on the financing track: MDB, bilateral, export credit or private
  • The gaps, in order, with what closing each one involves
  • A recommended next step, which may be to stop

How long it takes

Two to three weeks from receipt of documents, including one working session with your management team.

How it is priced

A fixed fee, paid in advance. If the project goes on to business development or financial advisory with us, the fee is credited against the first stage.

Two routes afterwards

Clients that already meet the six requirements use financial advisory alone. Most start with business development and move to financial advisory as the requirements are met.

Start with the check.

Two to three weeks, a fixed fee, and a written answer to the question that matters: can this project be financed, and by whom.
