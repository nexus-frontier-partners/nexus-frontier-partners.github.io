How we work

Lenders decide in a fixed order. We work in the same order, so that nothing has to be done twice.

Six requirements

What global financial institutions check before they read the technology, and what we do about each one.

What the lender checksWhat we do
01

Sponsor and structure

Who will develop, own and operate the asset; the project company and the contracts between the parties.

We define the structure with you, find and qualify the sponsor where one is missing, and draft the term sheet between the parties.

02

Site and resource

A specified site, the right to use it, and the physical resource the project depends on: water, land, grid access.

We shortlist sites against the lender's own criteria and secure the rights and resource data before the feasibility study starts.

03

Host-government demand

A written request or expression of interest from the central or local government, or from the operating agency.

We work with the host government until the request exists in writing and fits its own planning documents.

04

Proven technology

Operating references for the core technology and measured data for any new combination of technologies.

We separate what is proven from what still needs a pilot, and design the pilot so that its data is usable by the lender.

05

Feasibility study and financial model

A feasibility study to international standards and a financial model that shows the project can repay its debt.

We manage the study, engineering partners deliver it, and we build the model the way the lender's credit team will read it.

06

International standards and a dedicated team

Environmental and social standards, integrity requirements such as fee disclosure, and people with project-finance experience who hold the board's mandate.

We set up the compliance track early and act as the project-finance team until the client has its own.

Five stages

Each stage ends with a decision: continue, change course or stop. Fees are set per stage.

1

Diagnosis and definition

What the project is, who owns it, whether it is an MDB track at all.

2

Pre-feasibility

Sites, sponsor, host-government request, outline economics.

3

Feasibility study

International-standard study and financial model, managed by us.

4

Financing process

Term sheets, concessional windows, lenders and investors.

5

Financial close

Documentation, conditions precedent, first drawdown.

How we charge, and what we do not do

Our fees are for work product: reports, studies, structures, negotiations. A fixed fee for the readiness check, a monthly retainer plus stage fees for development and financing work, and a success fee only on private transactions, written into the mandate.

We do not take fees tied to public decisions: budget allocations, lender approvals, tender outcomes. We do not lobby. We do not prepare or submit applications for government subsidy programmes. We do not represent lenders or investors in the projects we advise, and we disclose any relationship that could be read as a conflict before the engagement starts.

Engineering firms deliver the technical feasibility study under our management; environmental and social specialists run the safeguards work; legal counsel in each jurisdiction handles the documentation. We choose them with the client, per project.
