Privacy notice

What we collect through this site, why, and for how long.

This site is operated by Nexus Frontier Partners (provisional name), Seoul, Republic of Korea. It is a brochure site. It sets no advertising cookies and runs no tracking of individuals.

Contact form

The contact form does not send data to a server. It prepares a message in your own mail application, which you then send to us by email. We receive only what you choose to send: typically your name, organisation, email address and the description of your project.

Purpose. To answer your enquiry and, if you ask us to, to prepare a proposal.

Retention. Correspondence is kept for up to three years after the last contact, or for the duration of an engagement and the period required by law, whichever is longer. You can ask us to delete it earlier.

Sharing. We do not sell or share enquiry data with third parties. Where an engagement requires it, information is shared with the specialists working on your project under confidentiality obligations.

Site analytics

If we use a privacy-preserving analytics service, it records aggregate page views without cookies or personal identifiers. We will update this notice if that changes.

Your rights

You may ask what personal data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and withdraw consent at any time by writing to the email address on the contact page.
