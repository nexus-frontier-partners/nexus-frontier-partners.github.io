About the firm

An independent advisory firm in Seoul, set up to make climate infrastructure projects bankable for multilateral development banks and global financial institutions.

Why we exist

Korean companies have technology that the climate transition in Asia needs: in water, in agriculture, in energy and in the systems that run data centres. What they usually lack is the project around the technology: the sponsor, the site, the host government's request and a financial structure that a lender can underwrite.

The advisory market in Korea is organised around other needs. Development consultancies evaluate and manage public programmes. Engineering firms design. Accounting firms advise investors. Nobody builds the project from the first day in the form a global financial institution will finance, and stays with it to financial close. That is the work we do.

How we are organised

We are a small partnership. Our partners lead every engagement personally. Engineering, environmental and social, and legal specialists are brought in per project under our management, chosen with the client for the country and the sector.

We work in English and Korean, with host governments, lenders and investors in their own processes and documents.

Principles

The rules we work by. They are also the rules global financial institutions expect of advisers.

Independence

We advise the project, not a lender, a supplier or an investor. We do not take fees from the other side of a transaction we advise.

Conflicts of interest

Any relationship that could be read as a conflict is disclosed in writing before an engagement starts, and the client decides.

Fee transparency

Fees are for work product and are stated in the mandate. No fees tied to public decisions. Advisory fees are disclosed to lenders where their rules require it.

International standards

Environmental and social standards, integrity and procurement rules are treated as part of the project from the first stage, not as paperwork at the end.

Seoul, and wherever the project is.

We work from Seoul with host governments, lenders and partners across Asia and beyond.
